03 / Change 1: Dynamic Surge Pricing ⏱ 1:30 - 2:15 (45s)

Dynamic Surge: 70% Occupancy Rule

Maximizing fleet revenue while preserving customer price fairness

📈 The Business Rule

When a bus becomes 70% or more full, all remaining empty seats automatically cost 20% extra above base fare.

Example (24-Berth Sleeper @ Rs. 900 Base Fare):
• Berths 1 to 16 (66.7%): Standard Fare = Rs. 900.00
• Berth 17+ (>= 70.0%): Surge Rate (+20%) = Rs. 1080.00
• Total Fare = Math.round(basePrice * 1.20)
>= 70% Occupancy +20% Surge Fare
⚖️ Integrity & Fairness Safeguards
  • 1. Price Lock Guarantee: Passengers who booked prior to the 70% surge keep their original price.
  • 2. Dynamic Deactivation: If a ticket is cancelled and occupancy drops below 70%, new bookings immediately revert to the normal price.
  • 3. Upfront Transparency: Surge status and calculated prices are clearly displayed in seat maps and booking prompts before confirmation.
04 / Change 2: Female Co-Seat Safety ⏱ 2:15 - 3:00 (45s)

Female Co-Seat Safety Protection

Industry-standard safety mechanics with smart group/family exceptions

🛡️ The Safety Mechanism

In a 2-seat pair (e.g. 1A-1B, 1C-1D, or double sleeper berths L1-L2):

  • If one seat is booked by a Female passenger, the adjacent seat can only be booked by another Female passenger.
  • If a Male tries to book, the booking is rejected with exact client message:
"Sorry, seat 1B is reserved for a female co-passenger. Please choose another seat."
Blocked for Solo Males Allowed for Females
👨‍👩‍👦 Group / Family Booking Exception

The Exception Rule: If two passengers book both adjacent seats together on the same PNR (e.g. husband and wife booking 1A and 1B in a single transaction), the booking is permitted!

Implementation: The validator inspects the current batch request. If the adjacent seat is part of the same transaction, the restriction is bypassed.

Single Transaction Safe Family Friendly
05 / Change 3: FIFO Waitlist & Auto-Promotion ⏱ 3:00 - 3:50 (50s)

FIFO Waitlist & Smart Auto-Promotion

Automated seat recovery with gender-safety compliance and CSV persistence

👥 FIFO Queue (Max 5 Capacity)

When a bus reaches 100% capacity (0 empty seats), passengers can join a First-In, First-Out Waitlist up to 5 entries.

  • Issues official Waitlist Tickets with unique ID (e.g. WL-NR-103-01).
  • Enforces maximum capacity limit of 5 entries per route.
  • Persisted to data/waitlist.csv across restarts.
🧠 Smart Safety Auto-Promotion

When any confirmed passenger cancels their seat, the system scans the waitlist:

Scenario: Freed seat is 1B (adjacent to Female in 1A).
• Person #1 in Queue: Dev (Male) ➔ SKIPPED (Kept at #1)
• Person #2 in Queue: Sneha (Female) ➔ AUTO-PROMOTED!
• Generates new confirmed PNR & removes Sneha from queue.
Auto PNR Generation Smart Female Check
